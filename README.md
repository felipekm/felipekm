#### 🧭 How it works

1. **2-week diagnosis:** I map, with your team, the processes that consume the most hours and show where the gain is before any big project.
2. **Pilot on 1 process:** AI runs one real process end to end, and the result is measured.
3. **Scale:** once the pilot is validated, what worked goes to the next processes, in order of gain.

**Built for:** industrial companies · automation companies and integrators · SMBs · teams with AI-shipped software.
